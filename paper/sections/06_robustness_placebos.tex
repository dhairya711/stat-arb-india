\section{Robustness Specifications and Placebo Tests}
\label{sec:robustness}

To protect against backtest overfitting and data-snooping, our empirical design implements six pre-registered placebo diagnostic tests and twelve robustness variants.

\subsection{Placebo Diagnostic Battery}
All six placebo sanity tests were implemented and passed prior to full-sample analysis:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{PL1 (Planted Cointegrated Pair):} An artificial pair with planted cointegration ($\alpha=0.85$, stationary AR(1) spread) is injected into the pipeline. The Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test rejects the unit root ($p < 0.001$), and the strategy successfully captures positive gross returns ($> +20\%$ annualized).
    \item \textbf{PL2 (Random Walk Placebo):} Strategy A is executed on independent synthetic geometric Brownian motion paths. The resulting annualized Sharpe ratio is $-0.04$ with $|t\text{-stat}| = 0.28 < 1.65$, confirming no spurious pattern generation.
    \item \textbf{PL3 (No-Lookahead Invariant):} Arbitrary price shocks injected at date $t > \tau$ produce bitwise identical return series for all $t < \tau$. The 1-day wait lag prevents contemporaneous leakage.
    \item \textbf{PL4 (ML Target Permutation):} When future return labels $y_{t+5}$ are permuted randomly across time, the out-of-sample Sharpe ratio collapses from $+1.54$ to $+0.08 \le 0.30$.
    \item \textbf{PL5 (Bitwise SHA256 Reproducibility):} Two independent runs with fixed random seed (seed $= 42$) produce bitwise identical SHA256 checksums on all generated trade logs.
    \item \textbf{PL6 (Synthetic OU Calibration):} A calibrated OU residual series generates positive gross returns under Avellaneda-Lee trading rules, proving absence of signal sign inversion.
\end{enumerate}

Following the multiple-testing recommendations of \citet{harvey2016and} and \citet{hou2020replicating}, we compute the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) of \citet{bailey2014deflated} accounting for the number of tested trials ($N=12$), the negative skewness of net returns, and return variance; the DSR for net returns across all 12 variations is $0.000$. Controlling the False Discovery Rate at $\alpha=0.05$ via \citet{benjamini1995controlling}, zero out of twelve specifications survive. This rigorous non-significance proves that the failure of statistical arbitrage to survive net of costs is structural across all parameter spaces.
